How it works

Three steps. No billing code.

Step 01

Billing events in

Every new subscription, upgrade, downgrade and cancellation is recorded once.

Step 02

Metrics computed

MRR, ARR, churn, NRR and LTV are derived from those events — no exports.

Step 03

Slice and compare

Filter by plan, channel or country and compare any two periods.
