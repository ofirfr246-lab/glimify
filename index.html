    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Something else on your mind? Join the waitlist and reply to our first email — it goes straight to the founder.

    What is Glimify?

    An all-in-one platform for selling a SaaS: hosted pricing pages and checkout, subscriptions and billing, a customer portal, and a real-time admin dashboard for visitors, checkout, MRR and revenue. Think of it as a storefront and control room built specifically for subscription software.

    Can I use it today?

    Not yet. Glimify is in development. It will run on our own product, MarketScope, first; then we'll open early access to the waitlist in small batches.

    How much will it cost?

    It's free during early access. Paid plans haven't been set yet — we'll publish them before early access ends, and you'll choose whether to continue.

    Do I need a Stripe account?

    Stripe is our first planned integration, for importing existing subscriptions and processing payments. Live visitor analytics will work with just the snippet.

    Are the numbers on this page real?

    No. Every dashboard on this site is a product preview filled with example data for a fictional company. We don't have customers yet, and we won't pretend otherwise.

    What happens to my email?

    We'll use it only to tell you about early access and the launch. No spam, no selling it — and you can ask us to delete it anytime. See the privacy policy draft.
